What the Needs Analysis Captures

The questionnaire provides a programme-wide view of educator readiness while retaining enough context to understand patterns by school, district, phase, subject and teaching role.

01

Teaching & School Context

Educator profile, school environment, position, subject responsibilities, teaching experience and contextual information for meaningful interpretation.

02

Digital Teaching Readiness

Infrastructure access, digital competency, classroom technology integration, online assessment, LMS use and AI readiness.

03

Professional Growth & Support

Training priorities, preferred learning modes, barriers, leadership potential, expected outcomes and individual development goals.

Questionnaire Coverage

The Needs Analysis moves from educator profile information into a structured set of ICT, teaching, AI, professional development and support dimensions.

Section A

Teacher Profile

Full name, employee/PERSAL number, school, district, position, subjects taught, phase, teaching experience, age group and school location.

Section B

ICT Infrastructure & Access

Computer labs, educator and learner devices, interactive technology, internet, Wi-Fi, LMS access, ICT Committee functionality and learner device access.

Section C

Digital Competency Self-Assessment

Computer operations, Microsoft tools, file management, communication, internet research, classroom integration, digital assessment and LMS competency.

Section D

Technology Integration in Teaching

Frequency of tool use, classroom applications, collaboration, problem-solving, learner-created content and confidence facilitating technology-enabled lessons.

Section E

Artificial Intelligence Readiness & AI Pedagogy

AI understanding, lesson planning, prompting, evaluation of AI-generated content, ethical use, learner safety, confidence, training priorities and concerns.

Section F

Professional Development Needs

Microsoft applications, SharePoint, Copilot, digital lesson design, online assessment, blended learning, Coding and Robotics, LMS platforms and content-creation tools.

Section G

Training Preferences

Preferred training mode and duration, including workshops, live online sessions, self-paced learning, PLCs, peer coaching and hybrid learning.

Section H

Barriers to ICT Integration

Device limitations, connectivity, ICT skills, technical support, time, confidence, leadership support, training opportunities and curriculum constraints.

Section I

ICT Leadership & Championship

Identifies educators who support colleagues, are willing to mentor, facilitate ICT workshops or serve as ICT Champions.

Section J

Expected Outcomes

Learner engagement, performance, preparation efficiency, digital assessment, digital citizenship, collaboration, AI integration and classroom management.

Section K

Individual Learning Development Plan

Short-, medium- and long-term development goals across 3-month, 6-month and 12-month periods.

Subject-Specific Follow-Up

Subject selection allows the Needs Analysis to collect curriculum-specific information where relevant, helping the programme understand which digital tools, teaching areas and subject practices require focused support.

MathematicsTechnology confidence, tools such as GeoGebra and Desmos, and priority mathematics topics requiring support.
LanguagesDigital reading, audio, storytelling, writing applications, AI-supported writing and language-teaching support priorities.
ScienceSimulations, virtual laboratories, data logging, videos, digital experiments, online research and difficult-to-teach topics.
Coding & RoboticsCoding concepts, robotics programming, computational thinking, assessment, classroom management and practical activities.
EMS, Economics & Business StudiesDigital resources for entrepreneurship, financial literacy, business planning, accounting and economics, plus tools currently used.
TechnologySupport across the Design Process, Systems and Control, Structures, Mechanical Systems, Electrical Systems and Graphic Communication.
